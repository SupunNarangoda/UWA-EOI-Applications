# Micro-Electromechanical Systems for Manipulation of Terahertz Waves — PhD and Masters

The project requires MEMS components that physically move to scale wave manipulation across the entire THz spectrum. My mechatronics-software combination suits this hardware-centric work.

## 1. Scalable MEMS Device Architecture & Micro-Fabrication Design

**The Research Challenge:**
Research and demonstrate MEMS devices using a "sufficiently generic" fabrication technology spanning the broad terahertz band — scalable, low-cost, and miniature — via micro-machining at the **ANFF WA Node**.

- **Manufacturing Precision**: **A+ in Introduction to Manufacturing Processes** and **A+ in Praxis/Design Project** give command over structural tolerances and micro-fabrication design rules for micro-lithography, structural etching, and wafer-level packaging.
- **Topology Optimization**: Current RMIT Honours focus on **Structural Optimization** plus a **DI in Mechanical Design 1** support designing micro-scale flexures and cantilever arms that resist fatigue and hysteresis under billions of actuation cycles.
- **Computational CAD Edge**: A **First-Class Honours Computer Science degree** enables programmatic MEMS architecture generation — Python scripts to parameterize layout matrices and run automated FEA to verify scaling before cleanroom fabrication.

## 2. Multi-Dimensional Wave Manipulation (Spectral, Spatial, and Temporal)

**The Research Challenge:**
The MEMS device must perform **spectral tuning**, **spatial steering**, and **temporal modulation** of terahertz waves.

- **Feedback Dynamics**: **Automatic Control Systems (DI, 78 WAM)** and **Control Systems Engineering (A-)** support building closed-loop transfer functions and state-space controllers that suppress vibration during high-speed switching.
- **Signal Processing**: **Signals and Systems (B-)** and **Advanced Math for Engineers** provide Fourier analysis, Laplace transforms, and complex variable calculus to analyze wave phase shifts and insertion losses.
- **Autonomous Edge**: **Agentic AI and Autonomous Systems** and **Autonomous Systems (DI, 76 WAM)** enable a localized agentic framework where each MEMS cell self-recalibrates actuation voltages against cross-talk and thermal drift.

## 3. Terahertz Spectroscopic Imaging, Target Recognition & Applied Data Streams

**The Research Challenge:**
Deploy the MEMS platform for terahertz spectroscopic imaging, target recognition, and chemical composition detection.

- **Digital Infrastructure**: **A- in Digital Fundamentals** supports high-speed data acquisition from MEMS diagnostic boards.
- **Deep Learning & Vision**: Training in **Deep Learning and Computer Vision** enables custom CNNs/spatial transformers to process and de-noise complex terahertz image matrices.
- **Hackathon-Proven Prototyping**: Winning the **2025 RMIT Gen AI & Cybersecurity Hackathon** demonstrates rapid development of pattern-recognition algorithms from raw data under deadline pressure.

## 4. High-Bandwidth Communications Architecture & Systems Interfacing

**The Research Challenge:**
Deploy spectrally tunable MEMS devices for future high-bandwidth networks such as 6G.

- **Firmware Integration**: **A in Embedded Systems Design Project** and **B+ in Electronics Circuits** support building microcontroller nodes and custom PCBs to route high-frequency signals to MEMS actuators.
- **Software Architecture**: Computer Science and AI training in computational complexity, memory management, and high-performance threading optimizes the hardware-to-software layer for ultra-low latency.
