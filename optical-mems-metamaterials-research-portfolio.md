- **Advanced Optical Array Optimization**: I am currently enrolled in **Structural Optimization** and achieved a **DI in Mechanical Design 1** at RMIT. This allows me to apply optimization techniques to design micro-flexures that remain flat during movement, preserving the optical phase distribution across the metasurface array.

**My Computational Co-Design Edge**

- **Algorithmic Optical Array Synthesis**: Manually designing thousands of unique nano-pillars across an optical metasurface is mathematically demanding. Backed by my **First-Class Computer Science Degree**, I can write Python scripts to parameterize, generate, and arrange large matrices of nano-structures. I can link these scripts directly to finite element analysis (FEA) packages to simulate how physical movement alters optical polarization and phase before starting fabrication work.
