# Research Alignment

## 🔬 1. Nanotechnology Metasurface Co-Design & Cleanroom Fabrication

### Challenge

Traditional optical systems rely on thick, curved glass lenses. Meta-optics replaces these with flat arrays of subwavelength nano-pillars capable of manipulating light through engineered phase distributions. These nanostructures must be integrated onto moving MEMS membranes and fabricated using high-precision cleanroom processes at the Western Australian Node of the Australian National Fabrication Facility (ANFF).

Relevant coursework includes:
