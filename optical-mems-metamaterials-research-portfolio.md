# Next Generation Optical MEMS Technologies Complemented with Metamaterial Functionalities — PhD and Masters

### 1. Nanotechnology Metasurface Co-Design & Cleanroom Fabrication

**The Challenge:**
Meta-optics uses flat nano-pillar arrays on moving MEMS membranes to focus light, requiring high-precision cleanroom fabrication at the **ANFF** facility.

- **Hardware Baseline**: High grades in manufacturing and design projects prove an understanding of structural tolerances needed for electron-beam lithography and etching.
- **Structural Optimization**: Current coursework in optimization enables the design of micro-flexures that remain flat during movement to preserve the optical phase.
- **Computational Edge**: A Computer Science background allows writing Python scripts to auto-generate massive nano-structure matrices and link them directly to FEA simulation tools.

### 2. Real-Time Programmable Control for Dynamic Reconfigurability

**The Challenge:**
Holography and remote imaging require dynamic, real-time programming of light by moving MEMS actuators rapidly and with sub-nanometer accuracy.

- **Control Feedback**: Top marks in control engineering provide the skills to model transfer functions and build state-space controllers that eliminate physical actuator overshoot.
- **Signal Transformations**: A background in signals and advanced calculus provides the mathematical foundation, like Fourier analysis, to map physical movements to optical changes.
- **Autonomous Edge**: Training in Agentic AI allows for creating self-tuning software agents that autonomously correct local optical distortions and thermal drift in real time.

### 3. Computational Holography, Data Processing & Real-Time Image Processing

**The Challenge:**
Deploying active meta-devices into real-world applications requires high-performance computational pipelines to reconstruct data and process frames without bottlenecks.

- **Digital Logic**: High marks in digital fundamentals ensure a strong grasp of the hardware logic needed to synchronize data acquisition boards with optical sensors.
- **Vision Pipelines**: Deep learning and computer vision training enable building neural networks that accelerate non-linear holographic calculations and clear up lens image artifacts.
- **Prototyping Validation**: Winning a 2025 Gen AI and Cybersecurity Hackathon proves the capability to rapidly build working machine learning models under tight laboratory deadlines.

### 4. Complete Research Footprint & Algorithmic Explainability

**The Challenge:**
Conducting research within an **ARC Centre of Excellence** demands high autonomy, independent execution, and peer-reviewed scientific publishing.

- **Research Tracks**: Completion of a software research project and a mechatronics capstone proves the ability to manage independent, dual-disciplinary research and literature reviews.
